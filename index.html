SilentSum

A calculator that works like a calculator, with an encrypted vault for your photos and videos behind it. Everything is encrypted on your phone, before anything leaves it.

On your home screen it is called Calculator, and that is what it is: a working calculator, used by people who want somewhere private to keep a handful of pictures without a padlock icon announcing it to anyone who picks up the phone.

It is a real calculator

Not a shell around a vault. It adds, subtracts, multiplies and divides, it keeps its result when you rotate the phone, and if you never set up a vault it stays a calculator for good. Nothing on the screen, in the app list or in the launcher suggests otherwise.

The vault opens with a sum

There is no hidden gesture and no secret long-press. You choose a PIN when you set the vault up, and from then on you open it by making the calculator show that number. Type it, press =, and the vault opens instead of a result.

You can also arrive at it in pieces. Any result that is a run of digits inside your PIN counts towards it, so the number never has to be typed in the open:

6 + 6 = 12 part of the PIN 1000 + 214 = 1214 more of it 2900 + 7 = 2907 the vault opens 7 × 7 = 49 just 49

A sum that has nothing to do with your PIN is only ever a sum. Wrong guesses count as failed attempts and the wait between tries grows, so the keypad is not a free guessing machine.

How your files are protected

  • Your key is derived from your PIN with Argon2id, which is deliberately slow and memory-hungry, so guessing it offline is expensive rather than instant.
  • Photos and videos are encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305, which detects tampering instead of quietly decrypting altered data.
  • Keys live in the device's own secure store — the Android Keystore or the iOS Keychain — and are excluded from operating-system backups.
  • Vault screens are marked secure, so they are left out of screenshots, screen recordings and the recent-apps preview.
  • Importing a picture into the vault moves it in. It stops being an ordinary file in your gallery.

What it cannot do. Encryption protects your files at rest and in transit. It cannot protect you from someone who knows your PIN, from malware running on your phone with elevated privileges, or from being compelled to unlock the app. No app can, and one that says otherwise is selling you something.

Cloud backup, if you want it

Off by default. Turn it on and your vault is encrypted on the device first — the servers hold ciphertext and nothing else. There is no password-reset path into your files, because there is nothing on the server to reset them with. Keep your recovery passphrase.

Private messaging

SilentSum Pro adds end-to-end encrypted messages between SilentSum users, with photos and video. Messages are encrypted with keys derived from your vault, so they are readable on your device and nowhere else. There is no user directory and no search: you reach someone by typing the exact address they gave you, or not at all.
